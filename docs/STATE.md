**Phase 6 polish — Lane-1 RAG indexer (step 3 of 4, partial):** New package `androscan/rag/` (`chunking.py`, `embed.py`, `index.py`, `search.py`) building a per-app SQLite vector index of method-level chunks over the jadx decompiled sources. New `pyproject` extra `[rag]` (`fastembed`, `numpy`).
